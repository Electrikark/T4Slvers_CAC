# ITRC identity-recovery support

The identity recovery topic offers Identity Theft Resource Center support after
the two existing IdentityTheft.gov steps. It provides a direct call link, a brief
service description, hours in Pacific and Eastern time, and official source links.
This route helps people whose personal details were exposed or misused.

The support stays inside the identity topic. It adds no third general helpline
panel. It is available without JavaScript and appears when the recovery page is
printed. The existing topic search opens the same content.

The source data is `data/help-cards.js`; generate `recover.html` with
`node scripts/build-recover.cjs`. Service details were checked online on
October 24, 2026:

- [ITRC service scope, free assistance, phone, and hours](https://www.idtheftcenter.org/about-us/)
- [ITRC contact details and hours](https://www.idtheftcenter.org/contact-us/)

Call: 1-888-400-5530. Monday–Friday, 6 a.m.–5 p.m. Pacific / 9 a.m.–8 p.m. Eastern.
The description offers guidance rather than a recovery guarantee. Online source
verification does not activate approval of the broader recovery advice; its
existing draft status remains.
