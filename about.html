ABOUT & PRIVACY

A second look, with clear limits.

Tech4All is a workshop prototype for checking messages and finding a useful next step.

What the checker does

It looks for fixed English patterns, such as requests for gift-card codes, passwords, sign-in codes, remote access, and urgent callbacks. It also compares extracted contact details with a limited local reference list.

It can miss scams and flag honest messages. It does not open message links, verify a sender or caller, or know what happened outside the text you provide.

What the results mean

Suspicious
A strong warning pattern was found. Pause and follow the next steps shown.
Insufficient Evidence
The app cannot establish a contact match. Missing details, an unreviewed record, screenshot text, or an unresolved check can cause this result. It does not mean the message is harmless.
Details Match
Contact details match reviewed records, with no findings or unresolved checks. This does not prove who sent the message.

The current contact list is awaiting human review, so it cannot produce Details Match yet. A listed address never cancels a risky request.

Reading a screenshot

The screenshot tool reads English text from PNG or JPEG images on your device. Compare every word with the image and fix errors before checking. Missing words, especially “not” or “never,” can change the result.

Screenshot text cannot produce Details Match, even after your review.

Your text and privacy

No account, analytics, or message history. The checker does not upload or save your message or screenshot. Processing happens in your browser. Use “Check another message” to clear the current check on a shared device.

The site and screenshot-reading files must first load onto your device. The app does not promise that every page will work offline. Official help links and phone calls leave this site; those services have their own privacy practices.

Leave out passwords, account numbers, and real sign-in codes. Paper copies you print may contain private details, so keep them somewhere safe.

What this site can help with

Use the checker when you are unsure, the recovery page when something went wrong, and the learning page to practise. The site cannot recover funds or file a report for you. Its U.S. help links lead to official services.
