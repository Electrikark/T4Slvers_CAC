Let’s take the next step.

Scams can happen to anyone. You deserve help, without blame.

Sent money? Contact your payment provider now.

Use the number on your card or the app you already use. Ask whether the payment can be stopped or reversed. Getting money back is not guaranteed.

What happened?

Open every section that fits. You do not need to enter any private details here.

I sent money or gift cards

Contact your bank, card issuer, payment app, wire service, or crypto exchange right away. Ask about stopping or reversing it. For gift cards, contact the issuer and keep the cards and receipts.

FTC steps for each payment type ↗
I shared a password or sign-in code

Open the real service’s app or website yourself. Change the password, including anywhere you reused it. Turn on two-factor authentication. If locked out, use the service’s account recovery process.

FTC account recovery guidance ↗
I shared personal information

If your identity was used, visit IdentityTheft.gov for a recovery plan. If your Social Security number was exposed but you are unsure it was used, follow its data-breach steps.

IdentityTheft.gov recovery help ↗Steps for exposed information ↗
I gave someone access to my device

Run an updated security scan and remove what it finds. Ask the device maker or a trusted tech helper for help. Change affected passwords using a device you trust.
