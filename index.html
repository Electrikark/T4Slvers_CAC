      About this check What the results mean
      
              
        
              The app looks for fixed English patterns such as risky gift-card requests, demands for sign-in codes or passwords, remote access, urgent callbacks, and certain deceptive web addresses. It can miss risky messages.
              
        
                
          
              • Suspicious: a strong warning pattern was found. Pause before acting and use the next steps shown.
                • 
          
              • Insufficient Evidence: the app cannot establish a contact match. Missing details, an unreviewed record, screenshot text, or a request that needs an independent check can cause this result.
                • 
          
              • Details Match: contact details match reviewed records, with no findings or unresolved checks. This does not prove who sent the message.
                • 
        
              
        
              The current contact list is awaiting human review, so it cannot produce Details Match yet. A familiar or listed address does not cancel a risky request.
              
        
              Screenshot preview reads English text from a PNG or JPEG on this device. Compare the full text with the image and fix any errors before checking. Missing or wrong words can change the result. Screenshot text cannot produce Details Match, even after your review.
              
        
              Choose Text, Email, or Not sure before each check. The app does not open message links or verify senders or callers.
