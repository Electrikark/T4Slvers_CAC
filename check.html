        How can I ask someone I trust?
        
        
        
          
        Before sending money or sharing a code, ask someone you already know and trust.
        
          
        
            
        
              
        Call someone you trust
        
              
        Open your phone’s Contacts and use their saved number. Don’t use a new number supplied in the message.
        
            
        
            
        
              
        Ask for help by message
        
              
        I received a message and want help checking it before I act. Can you call me?
        
              
        
                
                
              
        
              
        
              
        Only this request is shared, not your message. The app you choose sends it and may keep a copy.

      About this check What the results mean
      
                  
        
                  A warning is guidance. A quiet result is not proof of safety. The checker uses fixed English patterns and a limited contact list that is still awaiting human review.
                  
        
                  Screenshot text cannot establish a match to reviewed records, even after your review. Missing words can change the result.
